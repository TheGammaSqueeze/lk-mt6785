Title screens captured from games that ship no artwork of their own, used as the art of their
box covers in the release's SD card package (tools/release/demo_games.py). Each was taken on a
Konkr Pocket Advance running the game and reduced to the console's native resolution; each is
under its game's licence (both MIT):

- `Super Sudoku.png`: Super Sudoku v1.1 by Raphaël Assenat (https://github.com/raphnet/super_sudoku)
- `Raposa do Sol.png`: Raposa do Sol v0.20 by tibonev (https://github.com/jflores82/raposadosol)
